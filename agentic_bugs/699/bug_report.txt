**Problem**

**Steps to reproduce**

```python

custom_executor = LocalPythonExecutor(["matplotlib", "matplotlib.*", "numpy", "sympy", "io", "base64"])
custom_executor("zip()")
custom_executor("len()")
custom_executor("range()")
custom_executor("enumerate()")
```
