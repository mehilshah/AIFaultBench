### 🐛 Describe the bug

When deploying the Qwen3-ASR model using vLLM and running streaming inference, the streaming output appears to be segmented into chunks rather than being delivered smoothly. In contrast, non-streaming deployment with non-streaming inference works correctly without any issues.

code:  
vllm serve "$MODEL" \
  --hf-overrides '{"architectures":["Qwen3ASRRealtimeGeneration"]}' \
  --host [0.0.0.0](http://0.0.0.0/) \
  --port "$PORT" \
  --api-key dummy

infer: 
https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/examples/speech_to_text/realtime/openai_realtime_client.py

wav: 

[left_16000_02 (1).wav](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/29592374/left_16000_02.1.wav)

asr result:
Final transcription (raw): 
language Chinese<asr_text>我不是不不是这样的，因为跟你们协商了，我十号他已经同。
language Chinese<asr_text>同意我十号的呀。

A single-sentence audio gets recognized as two segments.  How can I get it to be recognized as one single sentence?
